About our service

Quality Mobile RV Repair Services for Your RV

Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service prides itself on being the one-stop shop for RV owners in Menomonie and across western Wisconsin, including Dunn, Eau Claire, Chippewa, Barron, Polk, St. Croix, Pierce, Pepin, Buffalo, Trempealeau, Jackson, Clark, Rusk and La Crosse counties. Offering a full list of mobile RV repair services, from diagnostics and repair to appliance and electrical work, plumbing and leaks, roof repairs and seasonal maintenance, there is something for every RV owner. Winterizing, de-winterizing and on-site inspections are the services that keep your RV ready for every camping season, all done at your campsite, driveway or storage lot with a fully stocked service vehicle. A flat $120 service visit covers travel and the first hour, each extra hour is $120, and parts are billed separately. To find out more or get your repair scheduled, don’t hesitate to reach out at (715) 308-1833.

Frequently asked questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How much does mobile RV repair cost in Menomonie, WI?

Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service charges a flat $120 service and diagnostic fee that covers travel and the first hour of labor. After that, labor is $120 per hour. Parts and materials are billed separately.

What does the $120 diagnostic fee include?

The $120 fee covers your drive-out and the first hour of labor, so we can find the real problem and tell you what it will take to fix it. Labor after the first hour is $120 per hour, and parts are billed separately.

Where do you offer mobile RV repair?

We are based in Menomonie, Wisconsin and travel to RV owners across 14 western Wisconsin counties: Dunn, Eau Claire, Chippewa, Barron, Polk, St. Croix, Pierce, Pepin, Buffalo, Trempealeau, Jackson, Clark, Rusk and La Crosse. Not sure if you are covered? Call or text us and we will tell you.

Do you come to my campsite, driveway or storage lot?

Yes. We work at campgrounds, storage facilities and home driveways around Menomonie and western Wisconsin, so you do not have to move your RV.

Do I have to tow my RV to a shop?

No. Most repairs and diagnostics are done on-site with our fully stocked service vehicle. If a job is too big for the roadside or driveway, it can move to Jeremy's shop.

What RV repairs do you handle?

We handle RV diagnostics and repair, appliance and electrical work such as refrigerators, furnaces, water heaters, converters, batteries and wiring, plumbing problems and leaks, roof repairs and seals, structural fixes, and seasonal maintenance and inspections.

Can you winterize or de-winterize my RV?

Yes. Seasonal maintenance includes fall winterizing, spring de-winterizing and inspections, done at your campsite, driveway or storage lot.

Do you repair RV roofs, leaks and plumbing?

Yes. We handle roof repairs, seals and leaks, plumbing problems and structural fixes, plus appliance and electrical troubleshooting.

Do you offer emergency RV repair?

We offer emergency roadside assistance when feasible. Call or text (715) 308-1833 and we will tell you honestly what we can do and when.

How do I schedule an RV repair?

Call or text (715) 308-1833, or fill out the quote form on this page. We will talk through the issue and your location, pick a time that works, and meet you where your RV is parked.

Who will work on my RV?

Jeremy High, the owner of Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service, runs the business personally. You get quality workmanship, reliable scheduling and a customer-first approach on every job.
