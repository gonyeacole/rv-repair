Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service ("we", "us") collects through this website and when you contact us, and what we do with it.

Who we are

Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service is a mobile RV repair business based in Menomonie, Wisconsin, owned and operated by Jeremy High.

What information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • The contact form. If you use the form on our site, you may give us your first and last name, phone number, email address, and a message describing your RV problem and where your RV is.
  • Calls, texts and email. If you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and whatever you tell us.

The contact form on this site opens your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. We do not store form entries on the website.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your question, give you a quote, schedule and carry out service, send you an invoice, and keep business records. We do not use it for anything else without asking you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone except the services we need to run the business, such as our email, phone and payment providers, and where the law requires it.

What this website itself collects

  • This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics, and does not set cookies of its own.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel and loads its text font from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser loads the page.

How long we keep it

We keep customer information for as long as we need it to serve you and to keep accurate business and tax records. You can ask us to delete your information, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
