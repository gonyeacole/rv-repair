About Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service

Otter Creek Mobile RV Repairs & Service is a mobile RV repair company serving RV owners throughout Menomonie and western Wisconsin.

What we do

RV Repair and Maintenance, Wherever You’re Parked

Repair & Diagnostics

We find the real problem on-site, from systems that will not work to issues that keep coming back. Most diagnostics happen at your campsite, driveway or storage lot, and bigger jobs can move to Jeremy’s shop.

Learn more about repair & diagnostics →

Appliance & Electrical

We troubleshoot and repair the appliances and electrical systems in your RV, including refrigerators, furnaces, water heaters, converters, batteries and wiring.

Learn more about appliance & electrical →

Plumbing

We handle plumbing problems and leaks, water pumps and water heaters, and repair them where your RV is parked.

Learn more about plumbing →

Roof & Structural

We repair roofs, seals and leaks and make structural fixes, so small damage does not turn into expensive damage.

Learn more about roof & structural →

Seasonal Maintenance

Fall winterizing, spring de-winterizing and inspections, done at your campsite, driveway or storage lot, so your RV is protected when the season ends and ready when it starts.

Learn more about seasonal maintenance →

Emergency Help

We offer emergency roadside assistance when feasible. Call or text (715) 308-1833 and we will tell you honestly what we can do and when.
